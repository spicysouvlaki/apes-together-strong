# Content guidelines

The rule is **honest but sensible**: celebrate real achievements and name real harms, without
turning atrocities into mechanics. Every figure, tradition, hybrid and event follows these rules.

## Hard rules

- **Chattel slavery is never a tradition, bonus or mechanic.** Institutions built on coerced labor
  (e.g. the hacienda) may appear; their history note says so plainly.
- **No living people.**
- **No founders of living religions** as figures or events (e.g. no Laozi, Buddha, Jesus,
  Muhammad, Guru Nanak). Religions in play are player-invented. Spiritual figures are later
  teachers, mystics, builders or archetype roles (e.g. Rabia al-Adawiyya, "an angakkuq").
- **20th-century dictators appear only as dark events**: never a portrait, quote, bonus, unit or seat.
- **Oral-record cultures get archetypes**, never invented named individuals. Archetype names
  start with "A"/"An" ("A Thule umiak captain"), `archetype: true`, and bios start "Archetype.".
- **Legends** (myths and fiction) have `kind: "legend"` and bios start "Legend.".

## Accuracy

- Assign each figure to the age containing most of their active life: Ancient to ~500 BCE,
  Classical/Medieval ~500 BCE–1450, Exploration 1450–1800, Modern 1800–present.
- Tag the culture the figure is most associated with; note other heritage in the bio
  (e.g. Ibn Battuta: Arab tag, Amazigh-Moroccan in bio).
- Don't project modern peoples backward. Example: the Inuit descend from the Thule (from ~1000 CE);
  earlier Arctic peoples (Dorset, Saqqaq) are not Inuit, so Inuit have no Ancient-age figures.
- Native spelling with diacritics plus the common English form where they differ.
- Every bio (≤ 280 chars) and history note cites a `source` in the data file (not shown in game).
- Quotes must be real, attributed and public domain or short enough to be fair use.

## Tone

- Specific and concrete: dates, places, what they did.
- No caricature in text, art or voice; no "noble savage" or "barbarian horde" framing.
- Conquerors are described by what they did, including the cost to the conquered.

## Review

Each culture's content gets a pass from someone with knowledge of that culture before shipping.
Track reviews in the culture's entry (to add: `reviewedBy`, `reviewedOn`).
