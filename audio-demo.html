Audio audition

tap to start

Everything here is synthesised live with Web Audio in D dorian (no audio files). Dev-only page; nothing on it changes the game. Use headphones first, then your phone's speaker: the mix is tuned for the speaker.

Listen first

  1. The signature: press Offer → recruit. The offer should sound like a question that stops on E; the recruit, two seconds later, should answer it and land home on D. If they do not feel like a pair, that is the first thing to fix.
  2. A busy turn start: press Busy turn start. Up to five sounds in about three seconds; it should feel like one short phrase, never a pile-up.
  3. A typical turn: press Typical turn with the music on (pick an age below). Clicks and footsteps should sit clearly over the music without being tiring.
  4. The music: pick each age, then raise a few culture sliders. Each age should feel different; each culture adds its own instrument. Toggle Crisis: slower and darker within about three seconds.
  5. Everything else: the effect buttons below, grouped by where they occur.

The drone hum is annoyingdroneFor() levels in src/audio/ambience.ts
A busy turn is too long or too busyDEFAULT_MAX_SOUNDS, DEFAULT_MAX_SPAN_MS and the gaps in src/audio/planner.ts
Something plays at the wrong momentEVENT_CUES in src/audio/planner.ts (sim events) or src/ui/ui-audio.ts (taps and screens)

After any level change, run the mix review below: every effect should stay at least 8 dB over the music (interface clicks 4 dB), and the numbers say which one moved.
